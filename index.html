I'm a copywriter passionate about turning customer insights into persuasive messaging that encourages people to take action. Alongside my Computer Science (Data Science) studies, I've built hands-on projects in landing pages, email campaigns, sales pages, and advertising copy while continuously improving my craft.

Data science taught me to look for patterns in numbers. Copywriting turned out to be the same instinct, aimed at people instead — understanding what someone wants, what holds them back, and the words that help them make a decision.

Landing pages, welcome emails, full sequences, sales pages, ad copy, headlines, hooks, CTAs — I write them, take them apart, and rewrite them until the weak line is gone. You'll see that process in the work below: the rough first draft next to the edited version, not hidden away.

My portfolio focuses on the writing itself — the thinking, structure, and editing behind each piece. I use AI to explore draft options, then edit the final copy myself.

Process

How do you work?

The steps I use to plan, write, and revise copy.

1

Research

Understand the product, the offer, and what's actually being promised before writing a single line.

2

Audience Analysis

Identify the reader's needs and questions using available research; note assumptions when a brief is fictional.

3

Messaging Strategy

Choose the framework — PAS, PASO, Hero → Hook → Benefits → Proof → CTA, Value → Story → Offer — that fits the goal.

4

Draft

Write the first full pass, rough edges included.

5

Explore & Compare

Use tools, including AI, to compare draft options and surface sharper phrasing — then decide what actually fits the audience and goal.

6

Editing

Cut anything that doesn't earn its place. Shorten, simplify, tighten.

7

Final Copy

A clean, structured piece — ready to publish or hand off.
