# HarshiniWrites portfolio

Open `index.html` to preview the static portfolio. The portfolio presents four core services — Email Copy, Landing Pages, Sales Pages, and Ad Copy — with Audience research, Messaging angles, Headlines, Hooks, and CTAs shown as supporting skills. The writing samples preserve Harshini's original voice with light editorial cleanup.

## Before sharing the site

1. Open `index.html` and find `YOUR_ACCESS_KEY` inside the contact form. Replace only that placeholder with your real Web3Forms access key. Do not paste your access key into `js/script.js`; the form already reads the hidden field from the HTML.
2. Optional: open `js/contact-config.js` and add your public email and social profile links. Social links must be full `https://` URLs. Empty values simply stay hidden and do not affect the Web3Forms contact form.
3. Test the form before publishing: submit your own name, email, and a short message, confirm the page shows the success state, and confirm the enquiry reaches the inbox connected to your Web3Forms key.
4. Check that the sample product claims match the real client brief before reusing any copy as client work. Remove fictional offers, pricing, and features that are not verified.
5. The site loads Google Fonts, Font Awesome, and GSAP from external hosts; use an internet connection to see the full styling and animation.
